Employee Benefits Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for company-sponsored health insurance.

Employees receive annual paid leave according to the company leave policy.

Employees are eligible for professional development reimbursement up to 20000 INR annually, subject to manager approval.

Employees may participate in the company's retirement savings program.

Health insurance coverage includes eligible dependents according to the insurance provider's terms.

Employees should contact HR for questions about benefit eligibility.
