Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of the expense.

Original receipts are mandatory for expenses above 1000 INR.

Expenses related to business travel require manager approval.

Managers must review and approve expense claims before reimbursement.

Approved reimbursements are processed during the next payment cycle.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.

Employees must provide a valid business justification for every expense claim.
